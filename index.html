Walmart · Chat assistant DEMO

Tell the assistant, it shops for you

  1. Type the items you need in the chat.
  2. The assistant reaches Walmart over UCP (recommended), directly over MCP, or through Muse, and builds your cart.
  3. Say "place order". It creates the BirchStreet PO and checks out.
US Foods · Supplier API DEMO

Predicted PO with a live check

  1. You see a predicted PO for a few kitchen items.
  2. You click Place order. BirchStreet checks price and availability by API.
  3. You accept any changes and the order is sent to US Foods.
Grainger · Chat assistant DEMO

Ask for parts, get catalog matches

  1. Type the parts you need, such as "2 LED flood lights 50 W".
  2. The assistant pulls matches from the Grainger catalog with price and stock.
  3. Switch options if you want, then say "place order".
Sysco · Punchout DEMO

Finish the order on the Sysco site

  1. Pick items from your favorites or a predicted PO.
  2. Click Place order. You go to the Sysco punchout site with those items already in the cart.
  3. Place the order on Sysco. The cart comes back to BirchStreet as your PO.
Chef's Warehouse · JAB agent DEMO

The agent turns a prediction into a real draft order

  1. A predicted PO for Chef's Warehouse items is ready.
  2. The JAB agent goes to the Chef's Warehouse website right away, finds the same items, and reads live price and stock.
  3. It saves a cart and a draft PO. You review and place the order.
16 suppliers · UCP LIVE

Order from stores that publish a UCP profile

  1. Type what you need. The agent searches linens, housekeeping, restaurant supplies, coffee and kitchen stores with search_catalog.
  2. Real products, prices and stock come back. Add items and the agent builds a real cart at each store.
  3. Say "place order". BirchStreet applies policy, then each store opens a real checkout with tax.

                  Live UCP suppliers: product names, prices, images, stock, store carts, checkout totals and tax come live from each store's UCP endpoint, called from this page with the BirchStreet platform profile.
